Hello! I am a Research Associate with the Arisco Lab at Williams College. My research interests lie broadly in human health and environmental change. In particular, I’m interested in understanding how our changing planet shapes health and well-being — through the lenses of planetary health and infectious disease epidemiology.

I completed my undergraduate training in Public Health at the University of California, Berkeley and my master's in Global Health & Population at the Harvard School of Public Health.
